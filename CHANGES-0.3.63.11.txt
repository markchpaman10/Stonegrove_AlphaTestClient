STONEGROVE 0.3.63.11 — Poplar Bow, stump and Wolf Head

Poplar stumps now use the supplied 16x16 Poplar stump icon, replacing temporary Oak stump art. The provided stump is used in both snow states; snow accumulation/melting state is retained.
Added Poplar Bow with the supplied original 32x16 artwork (first 16x16 frame for item/held display). Fletching 30: 1 Bones + 3 Leather + 2 Poplar Logs + 1 Bow String. Creates one complete bow and gives 90 base Fletching XP. Requires Ranged 30 to equip; base damage 6, speed 0.9 seconds, accuracy 10. Supports normal bow combat, player/mercenary equipment and eligible Nagalian weapon selection.
Poplar Bow and Snake Fang Arrows are registered Fletching level-30 recipes in the shared Woodworking / Fletching menu. Snake Fang Arrows retain their existing ingredients and two-second poison behavior. Added Skills > Fletching > Open Fletching, which opens the recipe menu directly and selects an appropriate Fletching recipe position; at level 30 it jumps to Poplar Bow. Using a Knife/Dagger on Poplar Logs also opens the menu.
Added Wolf Head using the supplied original item icon. Fully skinning a wolf has a 10% chance to grant one Wolf Head in addition to its usual hide and meat. Partial low-level skinning retains its existing meat/tattered-hide rewards. Other animals do not yield Wolf Heads.
Wolf-skinning rewards use an atomic inventory trial: if the entire hide/meat/rare-head yield will not fit, no items or XP are awarded and the corpse remains unskinned. The rare roll is cached on the corpse through failed bag-space retries, preventing rerolling; it resets on respawn. Successful skinning cannot pay a duplicate head.
Cave Snake Armor (body only) now requires 1 Wolf Head in addition to 5 Cave Snake Leather + 2 Rawhide String, with a Needle, at Leatherworking 35. Cave Snake Helm, Boots and Quiver requirements are unchanged, per user clarification.
Shared online crafting validates all updated ingredients/levels and consumes them atomically through the existing authenticated timed crafting handlers. Updated active equipment/Poplar guides, recipe tooltips, skill unlock lists and help labels. Existing characters/items remain valid.
Original uploaded PNG bytes are retained. Prior Poplar trees/logs, Nagalians/Queen editor, tailoring, advanced equipment and online/offline features remain included. No production account database or character saves were modified.
