STONEGROVE 0.3.63.32 - Fishing content, native art scale, crafting quantities and skill balance

Steel weapon/tool icons use the requested solid black square, including Sword and Shield; their existing crafting/equipment rules remain. Cover Tent and all Shrimp/Crab/Lobster Pot icons and world placement use native 1x sprite scale. Other camp tents retain their existing scale. Iron Reinforced Leather Helm/Armor inventory icon frames swapped; equipped body/head slots and stats retained.
Ruby Staff uses the supplied image. Magic 30 to equip; Woodworking 30 recipe: 2 Cut Rubies and 2 Pine Logs, Knife/Dagger, 90 base XP. Spell bonuses +4 effective Magic and +3 damage; melee stats 8 damage, 1s speed, 12 accuracy.
Shrimp Pot uses uploaded empty/loaded frames. Fishing 6, 2-second placement/recovery, existing shoreline preview, 30-second skill/soak catch chances, up to 10 catches, inventory Empty releases pickable Shrimp. Recipe: Woodworking 6, 2 Pine Logs and 3 Rawhide String with a Knife/Dagger. Successful recovery awards 10 base Fishing XP per Shrimp; Crab/Lobster retain 20/45 per catch and cannot award twice for the same pot recovery.
Shrimp raw/cooked/burnt sprites; Cooking 6. Cooked grades D/C/B/A heal 4/5/6/8 HP. Swordfish raw/cooked/burnt uploads mapped per user clarification (all Swordfish, not Tuna). Fishing 40 with the Pine Fishing Pole or better, chance on high rolls; Cooking 40 whole. Cooked D/C/B/A heals 90/110/130/150 HP. Raw/burnt/cooked states stack and cooked foods fit the potion belt. Burnt heals 1 HP. Knife/Dagger fillets Swordfish at Cooking 7 into 6-10 Raw Fish Fillets, scaled by Cooking; Cooking 40 yields 8. Existing fish-bone byproduct retained.
Bonfires grant +20% maximum HP and Defence for 5 real minutes after leaving. Adding fuel refreshes duration; HUD displays remaining buff time. Each log burns in 20 real seconds and releases 5 Charred Wood. Online fire creation/refuelling now use the timed server transaction path, exact fuel/XP, retry receipts and rollback. Buff expiry is saved; initial online restoration reads the stored character baseline, bounded to the remaining five-minute duration.
New characters start with all existing copper tools plus Copper Longsword and Shield in the bank (Axe, Pickaxe, Knife, Dagger, Hammer, Tongs, Longsword, Shield). Existing characters and bank contents are preserved. Meat recovered by full skinning adds 4 base Skinning XP per meat; partial skinning adds 2, for 6 total base XP rather than 4. Hide XP remains, with full rat/wolf/cow skinning now 16 total base XP.
Religion Altar blessing uses 2x normal blessing XP, with normal blessing capped at 50 base XP and altar at 100 per action before race bonuses. Already-blessed stacks never award XP again; huge legacy stacks cannot produce billion-XP awards.
Right-click a crafting recipe for Make 1, Make All, or Make X. Make X opens numeric input (1-9999 crafts), Enter/Craft starts, Escape/Cancel dismisses. Batches retain per-craft ingredient, stamina, grade/burn and cast checks; exact limits apply to offline and asynchronous server recipes. Busy recipe clicks do not alter a running batch.
Fieldcraft Camp Fire unlock is 2. Rest grants 20 base XP with the existing 300-second cooldown. Skill XP earned within 100 units of the owned camp contributes 10% of earned XP (capped at 5 base Fieldcraft XP per gain). Server actions and client production reports are deduplicated across replies/retries. Iron and Steel Bars stack up to 100; loading consolidates ordinary unblessed bar stacks without losing quantities.
Previous builds and archives preserved. Developer/server and alpha builds aligned. Original supplied PNG bytes retained.
