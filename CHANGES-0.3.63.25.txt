STONEGROVE0.3.63.25 - Respawn at a closer deployed camp

Player death compares the nearest available town center against the player's actually deployed camp in the respawn map. If the player's own camp is strictly closer, respawn at a safe point near the tent. A town remains the destination when it is closer or tied. Another player/character's camp cannot become your respawn destination. Packed/destroyed camps are not eligible. Blocked spawn points use the existing nearby-safe-position search; an inaccessible camp falls back to town. Camp selection uses center distances rather than offsets introduced while finding a safe spawn.
Offline and dedicated-server overworld deaths use the same shared rules. For cave deaths, compare the camp/town against the cave entrance in the overworld map, leaving the gravestone and its protected contents at the original cave death position. The online client retains the server's selected respawn coordinates after leaving the cave, rather than overwriting them with a local calculation.
Gravestone transfers, blessings, timers, recovery and all earlier changes are retained. Mercenary town respawn remains unchanged. This selection compares destinations in the current respawn region; it does not teleport between authored world regions.
