CHANGES 0.3.63.38 — HOUSE ROOFS, RANDOM DOORS, INTERIOR LIGHT AND MOUSE ENTRY

Every new modular house has a complete pitched blue or red roof above the matching stone/brick triangular gable. The previous third/fourth saved roof choices were standalone gables, which left those houses roofless; existing maps retain their saved choice but now display the corresponding complete blue/red roof assembly. New placement selects between the two full roofs. One/two floors and both interior/wall types remain available. Roof/upper floor still hide only when the player is inside.
New house doors are randomly placed on the left, centre or right of the front facade. The seven appearance fields, including houseDoorPosition, are saved on the authored object. Existing maps without a position receive a stable coordinate-based default. Saving/loading/import/export and editor Undo retain the door position. Ground-floor windows move around the door, and the collision opening always follows its actual position. Player, enemy and projectile collision use the same opening.
Right-click a drawn door to walk through it and enter the interior. Navigation first approaches the front of the doorway, aligns with its centre, crosses the opening and stops inside. This prevents mouse travel from stopping at the facade or approaching through a wall. Works for all three door positions, both floor counts and houses placed between navigation-grid cells. Keyboard movement or another click cancels the queued travel as usual.
Occupied new-house interiors have natural ambient light at night and in dark areas without requiring a torch. Interior darkness is capped at 6%; the outdoor world retains normal darkness and light sources. The lighting mask uses the actual interior sprite opacity, preventing bright grass strips around the transparent edges. Existing exterior/legacy-house lighting is retained.
Editor right-click interior preview, Shift-right-click erase, Undo, random/fixed floor choices and world package export remain available. The player's interior is drawn before actors, with roof/facade/upper floor hidden while inside.
Includes matching developer/editor/server and alpha client builds. Preserves cursed enemies/bone ownership, hallowed ground, death recovery and the prior gameplay/performance work.
