STONEGROVE 0.3.63.16 - Placed crab/lobster pots and shellfish processing

Crab fishing/cooking unlock at 12; lobster at 30. Replaced instant Crab Pot fishing with inventory Place, a green/red shoreline rectangle and two-second placement cast. Invalid red clicks do not consume or place a pot. Pots hold up to 10 of their species. Every 30 seconds of active world time they attempt a catch; odds rise with Fishing skill and soak time. Loaded server rooms continue pot updates when empty; no offline catch time while the game/server is closed.
Left-click your placed pot to recover through a two-second cast. Recovery preserves catches in the physical inventory item and refuses full bags without losing the pot/catches. Loaded frame appears in inventory. Right-click Empty drops each crab/lobster as a pickable ground item and leaves a reusable empty pot.
Right-click Crab/Lobster > Fillet with a knife at Cooking 12/30. A two-second cast gives one raw meat and creates separate owner-bound world parts. Left-click parts to break them or right-click Harvest; a two-second harvest gives a cached random 1-5 raw meat. Repeating an action cannot duplicate catches or harvests. Original uploaded crab/pot/lobster/parts/meat art used with nearest sampling.
Raw, burnt and each cooked grade of crab/lobster meat stack. Cooking stove/campfire grades and burns retained. Crab cooked D/C/B/A heals 12/16/20/24 HP; lobster 70/85/100/115 HP. Burnt heals 1. Cooked shellfish can be equipped to the existing potion belt; equipping moves exactly one portion from a stack, and use respects maximum HP.
Online placement/recovery/empty/fillet/harvest/belt consumption use authoritative transactions, cast-time/movement/range checks, replay receipts and persisted character revisions. Placed pots/parts are checkpointed with shared-world state; fallback saved placement records restore owner objects without duplicates. Pot item payloads are protected against ordinary-save catch forgery. Merchant sales/buyback of pots also use the existing authoritative material transaction path.
Fishing skill guide and help updated. Pot woodworking recipes: Crab Pot level12, Lobster Pot level30, each 3 Oak Logs + 3 Rawhide String using a Knife/Dagger. Old direct pot fishing and old prepare-meat recipe replaced by the placed/fillet flow. Previous releases preserved; developer and alpha builds updated together.
