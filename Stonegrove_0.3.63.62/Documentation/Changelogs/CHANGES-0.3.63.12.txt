STONEGROVE 0.3.63.12 — Crab pots, Oak/Poplar fishing and level-scaled cooking

Added all six supplied original PNG assets, nearest filtering and correct 16x16 frames: raw/cooked/burnt Crab Meat, Crab, empty/loaded Crab Pot, three-frame Oak and Poplar fishing poles, raw/cooked/burnt Manta Ray.
Oak Fishing Pole requires Fishing 5 and catches Large Fish, or Eels from Fishing 10; never Small Fish. Poplar Fishing Pole requires Fishing 20 and catches Manta Ray. Original Fishing Pole retains existing Small/Large Fish and Eel progression.
Equip the desired fishing tool and click a nearby valid shoreline fishing spot. Equipped fishing tools take priority; otherwise an eligible carried pole is selected (Poplar, Oak, original, then Crab Pot). Gates, authored markers, reach, timed repeating catches, movement cancellation, missing tools and inventory limits are enforced by shared client/server rules.
Crab Pot requires Fishing 30. Each successful haul yields 1 through the level-based cap: min(10, 1 + floor((Fishing - 30) * 9 / 69)), starting at 1 at level 30 and reaching up to 10 at 99. Catches give 45 base Fishing XP per crab. Pot is reusable, empty before a catch and shown loaded after a successful catch. This is a timed shoreline fishing tool, not a persistent placed structure.
Poplar catches give 35 base Fishing XP per Manta Ray. Oak preserves Large Fish/Eel XP (14/20).
Tools can be crafted with a Copper Knife/Dagger in Woodworking / Fletching: Oak Fishing Pole at Woodworking 5 (1 Oak Logs + 1 Rawhide String); Poplar at Woodworking 20 (1 Poplar Logs + 1 Rawhide String); Crab Pot at Woodworking 30 (3 Oak Logs + 3 Rawhide String). Fisherman tool stocks also include all three tools.
Prepare Crab Meat in Woodworking / Fletching at Cooking 30: 1 Crab with a Copper Knife/Dagger produces 1 raw Crab Meat. Cook Crab Meat at Cooking 30 and Manta Ray at Cooking 20 at a stove or campfire. Existing grade/burn rules retained; cooking XP is 90/60 base respectively.
Cooked Manta Ray healing by D/C/B/A grade: 30/40/50/60 HP. Cooked Crab Meat healing: 70/85/100/115 HP. Burnt variants heal 1 HP and sell for 1 copper. Eating and tooltips use the same food table. These match the existing level-20 and level-25 cooking progression while giving level-30 Crab Meat the next tier.
Successful catches check space for the entire yield before changing inventory or awarding XP. Tool recipes/preparation use atomic inventory trials. Skill guide and help updated. Prior version sources/releases retained.
