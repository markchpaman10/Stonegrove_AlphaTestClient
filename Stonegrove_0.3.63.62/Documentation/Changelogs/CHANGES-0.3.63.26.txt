STONEGROVE 0.3.63.26 - Black tailoring, Death's Robes and undead artwork

Black Dye: Tailoring 14, 6 Black Berries + 1 Empty Tube. Black Linen Cloth: Tailoring 14, 1 Linen Cloth + 1 Black Dye at a Loom. Black Cape: Tailoring 19, 5 Black Linen Cloth + 9 Wool Thread with Needle. Black Hood: Tailoring 20, 4 Black Linen Cloth + 5 Wool Thread with Needle. All four crafts receive D/C/B/A quality. Black materials stack to 100 per inventory slot with matching metadata. Empty Tube / Black Berries and Black Dye / Linen Cloth support Use-on crafting. Berry bushes can produce Black Berries, using the existing berry silhouette with a dark palette. Gather and regrowth retain the berry type.
All ten supplied PNGs are preserved exactly. Icons 1-4 and 8; animated cape, hood and Death robes paper dolls 5-7; Skeleton/Ghost body and head sheets 9-10. Directional frames use 16px source cells at normal game scale, mirroring right for left. Player, multiplayer equipment, mercenary and NPC paper-doll paths share the new equipment sprites.
Death's Robes are special Body Armor, not craftable or sold by ordinary vendors. Level-40-equivalent mage progression is set to 9 defence, +5 Magic, +4 healing. No skill or level requirement for players or mercenaries. Available to item/monster/NPC/quest/event editor pickers and authored rewards/admin placement. Explicit unrestricted metadata is checked by actual equip logic and shown in help.
Skeleton and Ghost are enemies, not selectable player races. Both have Level 10 base profiles adjustable through the normal spawn-zone and monster editors: Skeleton Melee/Strength/Health 10, Agility 8; Ghost Melee/Agility/Health 10, Strength 8. Skeleton drops Bones; Ghost has no default loot. No new immunity or special ability was invented. Spawn zones and editable monster templates expose both; enemy combat, respawn and server replication use existing shared rules. Enemies only appear where authored; existing maps have not been populated with unsolicited spawns.
Ghost is also an NPC appearance with an editable starter definition, selectable in the NPC editor, validated on export/import, and rendered in runtime/editor/event previews without human clothing/hair. Existing playable races remain unchanged.
Fixed an existing sparse-table serialization bug: Lua's length operator cannot establish whether slots are contiguous. Save/network packing now checks every index before using the compact array format, preserving later inventory items when earlier slots become empty during crafting, depositing or other actions. Both client profiles and the server use the corrected serializer.
All previous camp-respawn, gravestone, bag, quality, fishing and other changes are preserved. Update server and clients together. Existing production world packages need the new items/NPC/enemy definitions authored and exported where desired.
