CHANGES 0.3.63.39 — PINE-POLE MID/HIGH FISH FOODS

Uses the complete supplied 128x32 sheet: Swordfish top left, Manta Ray top right, Tuna bottom left, Shark bottom right. Each species has four frames: whole fish, prepared raw fish, cooked fish and burnt fish. Existing Swordfish/Manta item names remain compatible; their icons now come from this sheet. All four D/C/B/A cooked variants use the correct cooked frame, and all burnt variants use the correct burnt frame.
Manta Ray retains Fishing/Cooking 20; Tuna is 30; Swordfish retains 40; Shark is 60. All four are catchable with the Pine Fishing Pole (item name Fishing Pole) at their required level. Poplar retains its ordinary Manta catch; suitable higher-tier poles also offer Tuna/Swordfish/Shark. Pine Manta rolls 80-89 at 20+, Tuna rolls 90-99 at 30+, Swordfish rolls 100+ at 40+, Shark rolls 120+ at 60+ and takes precedence over Swordfish. Existing small/large fish/Eel boundaries and pot behavior remain.
These fish take longer to land: Manta adds 3 seconds, Tuna 4, Swordfish 5, Shark 6 to the normal level-scaled bite wait. The fish/XP are awarded only when the extra landing wait completes. The catch is held by the authoritative simulation, not rolled again after the landing delay. Moving away, losing the pole or running out of space still cancels fishing normally. Low-tier catch timing is unchanged.
Right-click a whole fish and choose Process Fish with a knife at Cooking 7. This consumes one whole fish and creates one graded prepared raw food: Raw Swordfish, Raw Manta Ray, Raw Tuna or Raw Shark. Preparation times are 2.5s, 1.5s, 2s and 3s respectively. It awards base Cooking XP of 20, 10, 15 and 30 respectively. Prepared raw items stack by their matching metadata/grade up to 100.
Prepared raw foods have dedicated stove/campfire recipes at their species Cooking level. Cooking times: Manta 2s, Tuna 2.5s, Swordfish 3s, Shark 4s. Existing whole-fish cooking is still available and now uses the same longer times. Existing knife-to-generic-fillet batches remain available and also use the longer species processing times; Manta/Tuna/Shark now support this option. Fillet yields at Cooking 1-99: Manta 4-8, Tuna 5-9, Swordfish 6-10, Shark 8-12; actual knife use requires Cooking 7.
All cooked foods have D/C/B/A quality. Healing: Manta 30/40/50/60 and Swordfish 90/110/130/150 (unchanged); Tuna 70/85/100/115; Shark 120/145/170/195. Burnt foods heal 1 and sell for 1 copper. Base cooking XP: Manta 60, Tuna 100, Swordfish 120, Shark 180. Existing race/activity bonuses apply.
New foods work with inventory/bank, cooking menus, healing and food-belt use; fish/seafood grouping includes the prepared inputs. Tooltips and skill guides show progression, processing and cooking requirements/times. Server timed actions derive preparation duration from the actual selected fish and cooking duration from the authoritative recipe. A client-supplied fish name cannot shorten processing. Inputs/output/XP are committed through existing camp transactions and rollback rules.
Includes matching developer/editor/server and alpha client builds. Preserves housing fixes, cursed/bone systems and prior gameplay/performance work. Update server and clients together because recipe indices changed.
