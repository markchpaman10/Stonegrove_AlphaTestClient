STONEGROVE 0.3.63.29 - Rune material quality affects spells

Blessed rune material quality now scales offensive spell damage and Healing Pulse healing: D 80%, C 100%, B 120%, A 140%. Offensive scaling applies after existing Magic, equipment and Religion damage bonuses; healing scales each pulse tick including gear healing. Existing ungraded materials retain C-equivalent 100% strength. Costs, cast chance, cooldowns and blessing XP remain unchanged.
Rune Bags preserve separate A/B/C/D counts within their existing 50-per-material limit, migrating old stored totals to C. Casting uses the highest available grade in the equipped Rune Bag first, then highest grade in inventory. Rune-saving bonuses retain the selected grade strength without consuming it. Failed casts do not consume materials. Deposit, withdraw, bank/save/profile/network item copies retain grades; full-inventory withdrawal is atomic. Mixed-grade counts and selection rules appear in Rune Bag hover details; rune material and spell-page tooltips explain strength. Melee Healing Pulse resets its rune multiplier to normal.
Both client and server use the same spell quality logic, and multiplayer carries Healing Pulse quality state. All previous content and the saved-cave restart fix remain included. Update clients and server together.
Rune Bags also expose their previously hidden blessed Cave Snake Fang storage slot and preserve its grades. Fangs do not grant a new spell.
