STONEGROVE 0.3.63.24 - Multiplayer gravestone recovery

Religion unlocks: no marker below10 (immediate public scatter), Wooden Grave Marker10 /20HP, Stone Tombstone30 /50HP, Stone Statue60 /200HP. Every intact grave starts with60seconds. Durability, highest blessing level and remaining time are independent values.
Death transfers carried bag containers and contents, equipment and its individual quality/sockets, equipped ammo containers and their contents, and potion/food belt into private server escrow at the death coordinates. Bank and mercenary belongings are excluded. Respawn uses the nearest safe town; cave deaths retain the grave inside the cave and send the player to the overworld town.
Left-click your intact grave nearby to recover belongings with a0.5-second cast. Current belongings are retained: newer bags use spare equipment slots where available; current gear goes into available inventory. If everything cannot fit, recovery is rejected atomically. Other players cannot retrieve intact contents. Right-click a grave then Bless to perform a0.5-second blessing. Movement or damage interrupts casts; a damage counter also detects damage followed by healing within the same frame.
The first blessing accepts any Religion level above0. Each successful stronger blessing adds Religion-level minutes to the existing countdown, without resetting time or restoring HP. Equal/lower blessings add nothing and award no XP. Successful blessing base XP is min(25,5+Religion*.2), passed through existing skill/race XP rules. The server uses stored/authoritative Religion progression for grave actions, not submitted level fields.
Nearby idle ordinary enemies attack graves occasionally (24px reach,8-second cooldown). Goblins and Nagalians deliberately approach within90px and attack every2seconds, while combat aggro retains priority. Grave damage is max(1,floor(enemy level/6)). HP0 or expiry scatters physical bag items, their inventory contents, gear, sockets, ammo and belt objects as public ground loot. Each grave scatters only once. Filled pots and nested Rune Bags/quivers retain state. A picked-up bag auto-equips into the first slot if the player has no bag.
Broken/recovered graves retain name, killer and blessing history for3hours. Hover shows grave type/status, HP, countdown, blessing level and last blessing player. Supplied regular/broken16px images are drawn at native world scale. The corresponding four-frame sheets play for0.8seconds after a successful blessing. Religion skill guide includes all three unlocks.
Server snapshots never expose protected contents. Durable shared-world checkpoints include private escrow, grave timers/HP/history and a per-character transition ledger. Death, blessing, recovery, grave destruction and public corpse-loot pickup trigger an atomic checkpoint before acknowledgement. Remaining countdowns are periodically saved and clean shutdown checkpoints them; restored rooms continue counting down without connected players. Stale multiplayer and character GET/PUT/camp saves receive the authoritative transfer state, preventing ordinary reconnect/save replay from restoring transferred belongings. Deleting a character releases its intact grave loot publicly and removes its transfer ledger, avoiding reuse by a new character in that account slot.
Caves containing grave/history objects remain accessible instead of being removed when cleared. Offline saves preserve grave contents and public ground objects, including cave state; cave-death saves occur after actual town respawn. Existing releases, artwork, worlds and unrelated gameplay features are retained.
