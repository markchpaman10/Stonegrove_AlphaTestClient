Stonegrove 0.3.63.61

Fixed stationary single-player main-menu camera routes on maps with no town markers or only one town. Such maps now get a local landscape sweep; maps with multiple towns retain the town-to-town tour. The camera starts moving after a brief initial pause.

Mountain ramps now exclude procedural resource and enemy placement across their full footprint, with clearance for trees and enemies. The protection applies to normal generation and resource/enemy zones, across all three mountain layers. Walking on ramps remains enabled; spawning on mountain tops remains enabled.

Verified the actual menu update callback under native LOVE for empty-town and single-town maps. An isolated server generated resource and enemy zones spanning a ramp: no nodes or enemies were on the ramp or clearance margin, enemies spawned nearby, and ramp walking remained enabled. Mountain-top spawning and linked cave roundtrip were checked as regressions. Lua compilation, character loading and ZIP integrity were checked.

Includes the earlier mountain styles, three layers, zoomable cave destination picker, held-click painting and organized documentation. Extract into a new folder for the clean layout.
