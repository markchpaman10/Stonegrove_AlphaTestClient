CHANGES 0.3.63.37 — CURSED ENEMIES, OWN-KILL BONES AND HALLOWED GROUND

All enemy families can spawn Cursed with a 5% chance. A cursed enemy is one level above its ordinary counterpart, receives the corresponding stat/health increase, displays the Cursed prefix, and constantly loops the supplied five-frame aura. Respawn resets the previous curse before rolling again; online clients use the authoritative server variant.
Bone-producing deaths now drop one Animal or Humanoid Bone Pile instead of individual regular bones. Cursed enemies drop cursed piles. Nagalians always drop Cursed Humanoid Bone Piles, including ordinary Nagalians. Skinning, companion kills and other death paths cannot produce a second bone pile for the same corpse. Existing meat, hides and other non-bone drops remain.
Separate a pile to obtain 1–4 random bones, ribs and matching animal/humanoid skulls, including possible duplicates. All eight normal/cursed bone parts stack to 15; piles are individual items. Uses the supplied regular/cursed icons. Own-kill outcomes are determined at death and retain their private claim through splitting, bank storage, ground drops and recovery.
Religion use is restricted to the credited killer's character (including their companion damage). The greatest credited damage contribution determines ownership; tank threat does not increase that contribution. Other players may collect/trade materials but cannot bless or bury another character's bones. Shop purchases, including buying your own sold bones back, permanently lose Religion eligibility. Legacy uncredited bones cannot earn Religion XP.
Right-click bone piles to Separate. Right-click cursed bone parts to Bless Cursed Bones; this blesses the selected stack once for 2 base Religion XP per bone. Unblessed cursed bones cannot be buried. Right-click parts to Bury Bone: consumes one bone and one Pine/Oak/Poplar/legacy log, awards 8 base XP for Bones, 16 for Ribs, 24 for either Skull, and places a Wooden Grave Marker lasting 60 seconds on clear ground. Leave at least 14 pixels between markers. These temporary burial markers are separate from death-recovery graves and contain no protected equipment.
Right-click a burial marker to bless it once for 3 base Religion XP. Any nearby character may help. Blessing does not refresh its lifetime. Hover shows the deceased bone, marker owner, recorded owner Religion, remaining seconds and blessing status. Online bone actions require a 0.5-second cast; moving or taking damage interrupts it. Religion racial/activity bonuses apply as usual.
HALLOWED GROUND requires AT LEAST FIVE live burial markers within 80 pixels. The nearest five owners' Religion levels, recorded when buried, determine the multiplier: 1 + average level / 50, capped at 2x. Four markers give no buff. Average 25 gives 1.5x; average 50 or above gives 2x. Expiration/removing proximity immediately removes the buff. Additional markers do not increase the maximum. Applies to Religion XP earned within the area and displays the current multiplier on screen.
Server owns the private bone redemption ledger, marker state and timers. Protected claims are absent from public snapshots. Character transaction failures restore inventory, Religion XP, markers and claim quantities; replay does not award duplicate items/XP. Server saves/restarts preserve claims and remaining marker time. Offline saves and region/cave state preserve their corresponding claims/markers.
Smithing exposes a disabled future bone weapon-enhancement framework with all eight normal/cursed materials and a Not in yet guide entry. No enhancement recipes or weapon bonuses are enabled yet.
Includes matching developer/editor/server and alpha client builds; preserves modular houses, existing content, recovery graves, shops, admin targeting and previous multiplayer performance changes.
