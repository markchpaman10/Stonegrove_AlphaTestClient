STONEGROVE 0.3.63.22 - Crafted item quality

All 167 implemented crafting recipes produce D/C/B/A ratings, with higher crafting skill improving quality odds. Ratings scale weapon damage, armor defence, bag capacity, food healing and potion effects. Normal crafted equipment/materials retain their recipe names and store quality metadata; matching rated stacks merge and different grades remain separate. Existing food and leather bag grades remain supported. Cooked chicken now has four quality grades. Direct bow stringing, arrow assembly, dyeing, slime tube filling, filleting, jewel processing, feather carving, tanning and leather processing also produce rated outputs. Quality is shown on item icons and tooltips. Save/load, bank, merchant, multiplayer equipment, tent/pot deployment and recovery preserve item ratings. Declared armor/shield defence now contributes to actual combat. Existing ungraded items retain base stats.
