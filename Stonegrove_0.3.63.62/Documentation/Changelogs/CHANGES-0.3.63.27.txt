STONEGROVE 0.3.63.27 - Surf and Turf and rare lobster tails

Surf and Turf unlocks at Cooking 30, using 1 Raw Lobster Tail + 1 raw Large Meat (the existing Large Meat item). Available at stoves and campfires. Quality D/C/B/A healing: 85/100/115/130 HP; 110 base Cooking XP. Uses the supplied cooked-meal icon.
Raw Lobster Tail is a rare inventory bonus when breaking whole lobsters. Every lobster in the bulk-break action gets an independent roll. Cooking 30 gives 5%, increasing linearly to 15% at Cooking 99, capped there. All lobsters still change into Lobster Parts in their original slots; no ground drops are created. Existing Cooking XP per broken lobster remains unchanged. Raw tails are not automatically stocked by ordinary vendors.
Cooking 30 cooks 1 Raw Lobster Tail into graded Cooked Lobster Tail, with D/C/B/A healing of 70/85/100/115 HP and 90 base Cooking XP, matching the existing level-30 lobster meat progression. Uses the supplied raw/cooked/burnt sprite cells. Burnt Lobster Tail and Burnt Surf and Turf restore 1 HP and sell for 1 copper; the latter uses the existing burnt large-meat icon because no separate burnt-meal art was supplied. Existing stove/campfire burn probabilities and Cooking quality rolls are retained.
Both foods support inventory eating, food-belt equipment and dedicated-server belt consumption. Raw tails, graded meals and burnt outputs stack to 100. Cooking consumes both Surf and Turf ingredients atomically, preserves ingredients/XP if output cannot fit, and cannot substitute cooked large meat. Recipe menus, Cooking unlock guides, tooltips, editor item/reward pickers and standard inventory/bank/save/network paths expose the new content.
If a rolled tail cannot fit during breaking, it stays attached to that Lobster Parts item as a pending bonus. The tooltip explains how to recover it. Harvest requires room for both the normal meat and pending tail; failure preserves the parts and bonus. Saved inventory metadata retains the pending tail, so there is no silent loss or forced ground drop.
Fixed inventory Eat actions consuming an entire food stack: eating now removes exactly one portion, preserving the remainder. Full health continues to block consumption.
All previous content and the sparse-inventory serialization fix are preserved. Both newly supplied PNGs are copied byte-for-byte. Healing and rare-drop values are explicit, adjustable balance settings in game/seafood_content.lua. Update server and clients together.
