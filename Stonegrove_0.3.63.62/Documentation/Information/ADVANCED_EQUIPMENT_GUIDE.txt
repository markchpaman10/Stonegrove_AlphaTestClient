Stonegrove 0.3.63.12 — Steel, Cave Snake equipment and Magic Weave

Why: integrate the supplied armor paper dolls, item icons, poison arrow, quiver, thread and metal loom into playable production/combat progression.

- Preserved all eleven supplied PNGs byte-for-byte in assets/advanced_gear_1.png through advanced_gear_11.png. Per user confirmation, image 9 supplies Magic Weave icons, image 10 supplies Cave Snake icons, and image 11 supplies the Steel sword/shield icons. Steel boots use image 8; image 11's extra boot frame remains preserved in the source asset.
- Steel Boots, Helm and Armor use image 1's four animation frames and three directional rows, with left-facing mirroring. Body and boot layers are cropped separately, so equipment slots remain independent. Steel Sword and Steel Shield use image 11.
- Steel Smithing: Bar 20, Boots 20, Shield 23, Sword 24, Helm 25, Armor 29. All require Melee Combat 20 to equip. Steel Bar = 1 Iron Bar + 1 Small Coal Chunk, smelted with Iron Tongs. Forge Steel equipment with an Iron Hammer and the recipe's leather/wood. The Steel Bar temporarily shares the existing Iron Bar artwork because no separate bar sprite was supplied.
- Steel Sword: 8 base damage, 0.62 second attack speed, 11 accuracy. Steel Shield: 8 defence. Steel Boots/Helm/Armor: 4/7/13 defence. Unsupported Steel tools/arrows and all Nickel metal families retain solid black Not in yet icons and cannot be crafted or equipped. This activates only the five supplied Steel equipment pieces.
- Cave Snake Boots/Helm/Armor use image 2's animated paper doll and image 10's matching red icons. Leatherworking levels 30/32/35; Ranged 30 to equip. Boots/helm need 2 Cave Snake Leather + 2 Rawhide String; armor needs 5 leather + 2 string + 1 Wolf Head. Requires a Needle. Defence 3/4/9; Ranged bonuses +2/+2/+3. Existing raw -> soaked -> stretched Cave Snake Leather processing is retained.
- Cave Snake Quiver: Leatherworking 30, 3 Cave Snake Leather + 2 Rawhide String, Needle. Requires Ranged 30 to equip. Uses image 5's distinct empty/loaded frames. Supports the same one-arrow-type storage, loading, unloading, drag/drop, bank metadata, reloading, mercenary and save behavior as the existing Quiver. It can hold Copper, Iron or Snake Fang Arrows.
- Snake Fang Arrows: Fletching 30, 1 Cave Snake Fang + 1 Pine Arrow Shaft + 1 Feather = 1 arrow (5 base XP). Accessible through the existing Woodworking/Fletching recipe menu. Requires Ranged 30; equip directly or load either quiver. Image 4 is its item icon. Successful damaging hits have a 25% chance to apply poison for 2 seconds, dealing 1 damage at each one-second tick. Misses and zero-damage hits cannot poison. Poison refreshes instead of stacking, expires after exactly two ticks, and defeats/drops only once.
- Poison damage and player arrows retain their originating player/skill references, including in shared multiplayer simulation. Arrow type is captured before the last arrow removes the ammo stack. Poison kills use the normal enemy defeat path. Mercenary ranged projectiles also carry their arrow type and can apply snake-fang poison.
- Iron Weave Thread: Tailoring 30, exactly 2 Iron Bars + 5 Wool Thread = 1 thread (60 base XP). Requires the Metal Weaving Loom. Image 6 supplies the item icon.
- Magic Weave Iron Boots/Hat/Robes: Tailoring 30/32/35, Magic 30 to equip; requires the Metal Weaving Loom and a Needle. Boots/hat need 2 Iron Weave Thread + 3 Dark Blue Cloth Strips. Robes need 4 thread + 7 Dark Blue Cloth Strips. Defence 3/3/7, Magic bonuses +2/+3/+4, Healing bonuses +1/+2/+3. Uses image 3's animated paper doll and image 9's matching silver/blue icons.
- Metal Weaving Loom uses image 7's full 32x32 artwork. Added to the World Editor Stations palette, authored station lookup, permanent world collision/navigation, minimap station markers and procedural town station layouts. Added beside the existing cloth loom in the bundled authored world. Clicking it opens Tailoring; the station recipes also remain discoverable in Skills > Production > Tailoring > Open Tailoring.
- All 16 advanced production recipes use atomic inventory transactions: invalid level, missing tool/materials, out-of-range stations or full bags consume no materials and award no XP. Smithing/Fletching/Leatherworking/Tailoring recipes award their own skill XP and existing racial modifiers. Online recipes use authenticated timed server crafting, station validation, durable inventory/XP and replay-safe completion.
- Equipment requirements, bonuses, recipe guides, hover help and saved item IDs integrate with existing systems. New materials/arrows stack up to 100. Existing saves, account data, Tailoring content, zone merging, partial skinning and all previous features are retained.

Update the developer/server build and all clients together. Distribute the matching exported world to include the new loom in an existing hosted world. No account reset or server URL change is needed. Existing custom worlds can place the Metal Weaving Loom from the editor.

Poplar Bow: Fletching/Ranged 30; 1 Bones + 3 Leather + 2 Poplar Logs + 1 Bow String, 90 base Fletching XP. Wolf Head: 10% bonus from full wolf skinning; required only for Cave Snake body armor. Skills > Fletching > Open Fletching opens the shared recipe menu.
