STONEGROVE 0.3.63.12 — Nagalians and editor-only Naga Queen

Added Nagalian enemies using all four supplied original sprite sheets. Armored/unarmored head and body are selected independently, allowing four combinations. Directional walking frames follow the supplied front/right/back rows; left mirrors right. The fifth image is retained as the appearance reference.
Nagalians randomly equip any implemented weapon eligible at their level, including melee weapons, bows and staves. Bows fire arrows and staves fire a basic magic bolt until dedicated Nagalian weapon art is available. Ordinary goblin weapons/loot behavior is retained.
Nagalians spawn in camps using the existing goblin camp structures, plus a bounded wilderness population. Both populations are active day and night. Their level and combat stats always use the map's maximum allowed level, including inside lower-level resource zones. Procedural camps alternate goblin and Nagalian groups; quest-created goblin camps remain goblin camps. Camp clearing releases caged animals and awards the existing camp-clear rewards.
Level Editor: added Nagalian spawn zones and Nagalian Camp markers. Authored Nagalian zones also use the map maximum. Monster Editor: added Nagalian appearance selection and preview.
Nagalian loot calls the same goblin drop function, with its existing probabilities and single-category limit. Loot is paid once. Corpses remain available for skinning after combat loot drops.
Nagalian skinning requires the usual knife and close range. Skinning levels 1–20 yield 1 Raw Cave Snake Hide; 21–40 yield 2; 41–60 yield 3; 61–80 yield 4; 81–99 yield 5. Each hide grants 12 base Skinning XP. Bag space is checked for the entire yield before awarding any hides or XP, and a corpse can only be skinned once. These hides use the existing snake-hide tanning/leatherworking chain.
Removed automatic Naga Queen generation and automatic server-restore spawning. Old procedural Queens are removed when persisted rooms are restored; editor-created Queens are preserved. The Queen is now placed through Level Editor spawn zones only.
Added a real draggable Queen level slider (1–100) in the spawn-zone inspector. Right-click a Queen spawn zone to edit it. Queen level is fixed to the chosen slider value, displayed in the editor, saved/exported, and used to scale combat stats/health. Default placement starts at 12.
Server snapshots preserve Nagalian appearance, weapons, level, corpse/skinning state and authored Queen markers. Developer/server and alpha builds include identical gameplay modules and original sprite assets.
No production accounts, databases, saved characters or earlier release archives were modified. Existing tailoring, advanced equipment, arrows/quivers/poison, editors, quests and online/offline flows remain included.
